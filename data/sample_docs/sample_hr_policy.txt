
COMPANY HR POLICY

Annual Leave:
Full-time employees receive 20 days of annual paid leave per calendar year.

Sick Leave:
Employees receive 10 days of sick leave per calendar year. Employees should notify their manager when taking sick leave.

Remote Work:
Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval.

Parental Leave:
Employees should contact the HR department for information about parental leave benefits.
